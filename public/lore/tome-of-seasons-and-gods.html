Wall vs game time

Real clocks schedule live events. Game sky, tides, weather, and gods’ days use the 6-hour day / 8-day week.

The Great Cycle

Time in the Grudge universe is built to be readable at a glance. A day lasts six hours, a week holds eight days, and four seasons complete the year. This makes festivals, raids, divine awakenings, and weather shifts easy to predict in lore and gameplay alike.

Bells of the Day

Each day is divided into six bells: Dawn, Rise, Heat, Ash, Dusk, and Night. These are used to mark patrols, rituals, and boss windows. Each bell is one real hour of a six-hour day.

Eight-Day Week

The week is long enough for travel, trade, and conflict without becoming difficult to track. Many holy days fall on fixed days to stay memorable. One week equals two real days.

The Year

Four seasons, each 96 days, create a stable rhythm for weather tables, religious calendars, harvests, and recurring world events. A year is 384 game days.

The Four Seasons

Seasons in Grudge are more than climate; they are divine moods that change the world’s hostility, abundance, and spiritual pressure.

1. Dawnfall Spring

Rain returns, graves soften, and old magic wakes beneath root and stone. The Fabled’s influence is strongest here, and healing omens are common.

Days of year: 1–96 · Omni-leaning

2. Iron Sun Summer

Heat drives warbands into motion. Sea routes are clear, battles are frequent, and Odin’s war-signs appear in lightning and banners of ash.

Days of year: 97–192 · Odin-leaning

3. Hollow Harvest Autumn

Crops, relics, and bones are gathered before the cold. Madra’s rites of transformation rise in power, and corruption spreads through weak villages.

Days of year: 193–288 · Madra-leaning

4. Black Frost Winter

Storms silence roads and sharpen the dead. Winter is the season of exile, siege, and divine judgment. The strongest prayers are made in this dark.

Days of year: 289–384 · Judgment season

Religions and Patrons

Faith is factional in Grudge. Each great power carries a patron god whose doctrine shapes war, law, magic, and the shape of holy days.

The Crusade — Odin

Domain: War, Wisdom, Fate, Victory. The Crusade teaches that honor is proven through action and that a worthy death binds a soul to legend.

Sacred timing: Oathday at Bell of Rise · Temples near the Waterfall’s edge

The Legion — Madra

Domain: Entropy, Transformation, Destruction, Rebirth. The Legion believes that all strength must be broken before it can become something greater.

Sacred timing: Ashday at Bell of Ash · Maw temples on fallen shores

The Fabled — The Omni

Domain: Balance, Unity, Infinity, Harmony. The Fabled preserve peace through order, consensus, and sacred geometry.

Sacred timing: Balance Day at Bell of Dusk · Sanctums on central isles

The Pirate Confederacy — Racalvin

Domain: Trade, theft, tide-law, and survival. They do not preach a faith so much as a code: coin obeys no throne, and every harbor has a price.

Sacred timing: Tide Day at Bell of Heat · Free Port markets

Events and Omens

These are the recurring lore events that can anchor quests, world changes, and seasonal threats.

The First Bell War

A yearly opening event where banners are raised and border fortresses test their strength.

Date: Dawnfall 1, Oathday, Bell of Dawn

Ash Tide Moon

A corruption season where undead rise easier, seas turn dark, and Madra-linked enemies appear more often.

Date: Hollow Harvest 63–70, Ashday through Night’s End

The Broken Crown Solstice

A midwinter omen when rulers, bosses, and gods are said to reveal their true names in dreams.

Date: Black Frost 96, Skywatch, Bell of Night

The Nexus Claim Feast

A pirate and trade event that turns hostile whenever territory is seized in the Nexus sector.

Date: Tide Day, Week 4 of any season, Bell of Heat

The Red Harvest

Villages, fields, and camps are taxed in blood or grain. This is a prime time for raids and monster migrations.

Date: Hollow Harvest 24, Gravesday, Bell of Dusk

The Godwake

A rare cycle when a god’s presence bleeds into the world, empowering temples, altering bosses, and changing weather for a full week.

Date: Any season, Year-turn + 8 days, Bell of Night

Format

Seasonal Rules

Weather tables change by season, boss spawn rates shift by moon, and holy sites become stronger on their patron’s day.

Yearly Events

Every year can end with a divine convergence, a world siege, or a reset of faction influence across the map.

Lore Timing

If an event needs a sacred mood, tie it to a gods’ day. If it needs a broad world state, tie it to the season. If it needs a rare moment, tie it to a moon turn.

In practice

Daily content

Rotating patrols, bounty boards, tavern rumors, and small shrine blessings.

Weekly content

One faction siege, one market day, one oracle event, and one hidden boss window.

Seasonal content

Weather shifts, harvest wars, blizzard sieges, corruption surges, and pilgrimage routes.

Yearly content

Godwakes, world resets, relic migrations, grand festivals, and map-wide faction changes.
