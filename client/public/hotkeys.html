3D Play — Weapons & Modes

Z Sheath / draw weapons (hip or back). Toggle anytime.
Tab Soft-lock cycle (yellow frame). Shift+Tab reverse.
I Inventory panel (ui.grudge-studio chrome)
LMB Attack (auto-draws if sheathed)
C Combat motion attack 2
X Combat attack 3 · climb detach when on wall
Space Jump · hold near wall to climb

Auto-holster: climb grab, swim-to-edge, build mode (hands free). Stamina does not regen while climbing.
