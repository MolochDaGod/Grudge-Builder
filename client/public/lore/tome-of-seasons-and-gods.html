Wall vs game time

Real clocks schedule live events. Game sky, tides, weather, and gods’ days use the 6-hour day / 8-day week.

The Great Cycle

Time in the Grudge universe is built to be readable at a glance. A day lasts six hours, a week holds eight days, and four seasons complete the year — so festivals, raids, divine awakenings, and weather shifts stay predictable in lore and gameplay.

Bells of the Day

Six bells — Dawn, Rise, Heat, Ash, Dusk, Night — mark patrols, rituals, and boss windows. Each bell is one real hour of a six-hour day.

Eight-Day Week

Long enough for travel, trade, and conflict without losing track. Holy days fall on fixed gods’ days. One week equals two real days.

The Year

Four seasons × 96 days create weather tables, religious calendars, harvests, and recurring world events. A year is 384 game days.

Practical time format

The Four Seasons

Seasons in Grudge are more than climate; they are divine moods that change the world’s hostility, abundance, and spiritual pressure.

1. Dawnfall Spring

Rain returns, graves soften, and old magic wakes beneath root and stone. The Fabled’s influence is strongest here, and healing omens are common.

Days 1–96 · Omni-leaning

2. Iron Sun Summer

Heat drives warbands into motion. Sea routes are clear, battles are frequent, and Odin’s war-signs appear in lightning and banners of ash.

Days 97–192 · Odin-leaning

3. Hollow Harvest Autumn

Crops, relics, and bones are gathered before the cold. Madra’s rites of transformation rise in power, and corruption spreads through weak villages.

Days 193–288 · Madra-leaning

4. Black Frost Winter

Storms silence roads and sharpen the dead. Winter is the season of exile, siege, and divine judgment. The strongest prayers are made in this dark.

Days 289–384 · Judgment season

Religions and Patrons

Faith is factional in Grudge. Each great power carries a patron god whose doctrine shapes war, law, magic, and the shape of holy days.

The Crusade — Odin

Domain: War, Wisdom, Fate, Victory. Honor is proven through action; a worthy death binds a soul to legend.

Oathday · Bell of Rise · Temples near the Waterfall’s edge

The Legion — Madra

Domain: Entropy, Transformation, Destruction, Rebirth. All strength must be broken before it can become greater.

Ashday · Bell of Ash · Maw temples on fallen shores

The Fabled — The Omni

Domain: Balance, Unity, Infinity, Harmony. Peace through order, consensus, and sacred geometry.

Balance Day · Bell of Dusk · Sanctums on central isles

The Pirate Confederacy — Racalvin

Domain: Trade, theft, tide-law, survival. Coin obeys no throne; every harbor has a price.

Tide Day · Bell of Heat · Free Port markets
