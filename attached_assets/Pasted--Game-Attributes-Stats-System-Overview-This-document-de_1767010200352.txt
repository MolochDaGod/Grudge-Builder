## How Stats Work

### Core Combat Stats

- **Health:** Total hit points
- **Mana:** Resource for casting spells
- **Stamina:** Resource for physical abilities
- **Damage:** Base attack damage (modified by equipment and level)
- **Defense:** Reduces incoming damage

### Combat Mechanics

- **Block Chance:** % chance to block an attack (reduces damage)
- **Critical Chance:** % chance to deal critical damage
- **Block Factor:** Damage reduction percentage when blocking (e.g., 0.3 = 30% reduction)
- **Critical Factor:** Damage multiplier on critical hits (e.g., 1.5 = 150% damage)
- **Accuracy:** % chance to apply debuffs/negative effects
- **Resistance:** % chance to resist debuffs/negative effects

### Advanced Combat Factors

- **Drain Health Factor:** % of damage dealt returned as health
- **Drain Mana Factor:** % of damage dealt returned as mana
- **Reflect Damage Factor:** % of damage received reflected to attacker
- **Defense Break Factor:** Reduces target's defense by %
- **Block Break Factor:** Reduces target's block chance by %
- **Critical Evasion:** Reduces enemy's critical chance against you
- **Absorb Health Factor:** Regenerate % health when taking damage
- **Absorb Mana Factor:** Regenerate % mana when taking damage

## Calculation System

### Formula

For each stat, the total bonus is calculated as:

```
Total Bonus = (Flat Bonus × Points) + (Base Stat × Percent Bonus × Points)
```

### Example: Strength with 10 points

- **Health Bonus:** (26 × 10) + (Base Health × 0.008 × 10)
  - If Base Health = 100: 260 + (100 × 0.08) = 260 + 8 = **268 Health**
- **Damage Bonus:** (3 × 10) + (Base Damage × -0.391 × 10)
  - If Base Damage = 50: 30 + (50 × -3.91) = 30 - 195.5 = **-165.5 Damage** (modifier reduces scaling)

### Notes on Percentage Values

- **Positive percentages** increase the scaling with base stats
- **Negative percentages** represent effectiveness modifiers that may reduce scaling in some contexts
- The system uses both flat and percentage bonuses to create unique attribute scaling curves

## Attribute Synergies

### Tank Builds

- **Strength + Vitality + Endurance:** Maximum survivability with health, defense, and damage absorption

### Physical DPS

- **Strength + Dexterity + Agility:** High damage output with critical strikes and accuracy

### Magic DPS

- **Intellect + Wisdom:** High mana pool with spell damage and mana efficiency

### Hybrid Builds

- **Tactics + Any Attribute:** Tactics provides balanced bonuses that complement any playstyle
- **Agility + Dexterity:** Maximum critical chance and evasion for dodge-based combat

## System Architecture

### Implementation Files

- **Attribute Templates:** `Assets/uMMORPG/Resources/Attributes/*.asset`
- **Attribute System:** `Scripts/Addons/Attributes/Scripts/Add to prefab player/PlayerAttributes.cs`
- **Combat Integration:** `Scripts/Addons/Attributes/Scripts/Lib/Attributes.Combat.cs`
- **Template Definition:** `Scripts/Addons/Attributes/Scripts/Scriptable/AttributeTemplate.cs`
- **Attribute Struct:** `Scripts/Addons/Attributes/Scripts/Lib/TrugordAttribute.cs`

### Bonus Sources

Attributes contribute to stats through the `IHealthBonus`, `IManaBonus`, and `ICombatBonus` interfaces:

1. **Equipment bonuses** from attribute modifiers
2. **Buff bonuses** from skills/abilities
3. **Trait bonuses** from character traits
4. **Set bonuses** from equipment sets
5. **Title bonuses** from earned titles

## Developer Notes

### Adding New Attributes

1. Create a new `AttributeTemplate` ScriptableObject in `Resources/Attributes/`
2. Configure flat and percent bonuses for each stat
3. The system automatically integrates new attributes through the template dictionary

### Modifying Existing Attributes

Edit the `.asset` files in `Resources/Attributes/` to adjust:

- `flatHealth`, `flatMana`, `flatDamage`, etc. (fixed bonuses)
- `percentHealth`, `percentMana`, `percentDamage`, etc. (scaling bonuses)

### Caching System

The attribute system uses caching with `cacheTimerInterval` to optimize performance:

- Calculated values are cached with timestamps
- Cache invalidates after the timer expires
- Reduces redundant calculations during combat
